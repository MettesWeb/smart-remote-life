What is Remote Rocketship?

Remote Rocketship is a specialist search platform for remote roles. According to the provider, it continuously scans company career pages, surfaces new roles early and verifies listings. The focus is on fully remote roles and practical filtering by location, experience and other criteria.

Early job discoveryRoles are collected directly from many company career pages.
Detailed filtersCountry, region, experience, salary and other attributes help narrow the search.
Alerts & matchingSaved searches, email alerts and resume matching add another layer to the search.

Key features

  • fully remote roles with location and time-zone context
  • filters for job title, location, seniority, salary and more
  • visibility into roles that may not appear on LinkedIn
  • email alerts for saved searches
  • resume matching with job-specific match scores
  • depending on the plan, resume/cover-letter tools plus API, CLI and MCP access

How much does Remote Rocketship cost?

As of October 2026, Remote Rocketship lists three paid options: €24 monthly, €48 per quarter or €78 per year. You can also browse jobs for free. The provider says the paid plans unlock the same features and mainly differ by billing period.

Pricing and features can change. Check the current pricing page before subscribing.

Who is it best for?

Active job seekers

Useful if you want to monitor fresh remote roles and react quickly to new openings.

Good fit: marketing, support, operations, tech, design, sales and many other digital roles.

International search

Useful if you want to filter by Germany, Europe or other regions instead of browsing one global list.

Important: remote does not automatically mean you may work from every country.

Remote Rocketship FAQ

Is Remote Rocketship free?

You can browse remote jobs for free. Paid plans unlock the full feature set.

What filters does Remote Rocketship offer?

Depending on the search, you can filter by location, experience level, salary and other job attributes. The platform focuses on remote roles and adds matching and alert features.

Is it useful for Europe?

Yes. You can narrow searches by country or region, but you should still check each listing for hiring-location restrictions.

Where do I apply?

Remote Rocketship takes you to the original employer source or application page.
