What is Remotive?

Remotive is a specialist platform for fully remote jobs. Listings are organised by category and can be narrowed with detailed filters. The combination of location, skill, seniority and employment-type filters is especially useful.

Curated rolesRemotive focuses on remote jobs and organises listings into clear categories.
Detailed filtersLocation, category, language, skills, employment type, seniority and salary.
Job alertsSaved searches and custom alerts help you react to new roles.

How to search effectively

Start with location

The “I want to work remotely from…” filter helps surface roles that can actually be done from your location. That is often more useful than searching only for “Worldwide”.

Useful for: job seekers based in Germany, Europe or another specific region.

Then narrow by role

Combine location with category, skills, employment type, seniority and salary to turn a huge list into a realistic shortlist.

Useful for: people testing several related job titles.

Membership and pricing

Remotive offers a paid membership in addition to public job browsing. According to its helpdesk, there are monthly, quarterly and yearly plans. Exact pricing can vary by country, currency and taxes and is shown during onboarding.

That is why this guide does not list a fixed price. Check the current amount directly on Remotive.

Remotive FAQ

Is Remotive free?

Part of the job search is publicly accessible. Remotive also offers a paid membership with expanded access and additional search features.

Which filters are available?

Remotive supports filters for location, category, language, skills, employment type, seniority and salary.

Are all jobs available worldwide?

No. Fully remote roles can still have country, region or time-zone restrictions.

How much does membership cost?

Remotive offers monthly, quarterly and yearly plans. Exact pricing can vary by country, currency and taxes and is shown during onboarding.
